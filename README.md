# Course Triage Wizard

A lightweight diagnostic app for online course creators to identify
why their course is not selling or not being completed.

## What this app does
- Guides creators through a short intake (2–7 minutes)
- Generates a Course Health Scorecard
- Identifies the primary bottleneck (sales, completion, clarity, or overwhelm)
- Recommends what to fix first
- Leads into the **Fix Your Course in 30 Days** program

## Tech Stack
- Node.js
- Express
- Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript

## Status
MVP – rules-based logic (no AI yet)
